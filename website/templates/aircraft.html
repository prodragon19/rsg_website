About the RC-3 Seabee

The Republic RC-3 Seabee is an amphibious aircraft from the 1940s. It was built as a four-seat flying boat for private and utility use, able to operate from both water and land.

With its pusher propeller and all-metal hull, it is one of the most distinctive general aviation aircraft of its time. Our addon brings it to Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.
