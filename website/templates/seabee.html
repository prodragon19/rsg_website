About the RC-3 Seabee

The Republic RC-3 Seabee is an American amphibious aircraft first flown in the 1940s. Built by Republic Aviation, it was designed as a four-seat flying boat for private and utility use, able to operate from both water and land.

With its pusher propeller, all-metal hull and distinctive shape, the Seabee became a classic among amphibious aircraft. Our addon brings this unique aircraft to Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 with high-fidelity modeling and authentic systems.

Features

  • Highly detailed 3D model
  • Amphibious operations on water and land
  • Custom avionics and systems
  • Realistic flight dynamics
  • Authentic sound set
